Film by Dust Wave Data Deletion

Updated July 10, 2026

Disconnect a provider

  1. Sign in to Film as the workspace owner or producer.
  2. Open the provider inspector and choose the connected provider.
  3. Select Disconnect and confirm the request.
  4. Film revokes the provider connection when supported and removes locally stored token material. Imported Film records remain until separately deleted.

Remove local browser data

Use the browser's site-data controls for film.dustwave.xyz to remove local workspace mirrors, preferences, staged attachments, and local operation queues from that device. Export or back up records first when needed.

Delete imported or workspace records

Use Film's supported record controls, restore workflow, or encrypted backup tools to remove or replace imported records. A provider disconnection does not automatically delete copies that a user intentionally imported into Film.

Request complete deletion

Ask the Film workspace owner to submit a complete account or workspace deletion request through the established Dust Wave support channel used for your invitation. Include the workspace name and the connected provider to remove, but do not send passwords, OAuth tokens, backup passphrases, or magic links.

Deletion requests are authenticated through the workspace relationship. Film may retain bounded security, audit, or legal records when reasonably necessary, and will delete or anonymize other covered data after verification.

See the Film Privacy Policy for retention and sharing details.
