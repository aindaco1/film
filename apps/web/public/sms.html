Film by Dust Wave SMS Terms

Effective July 13, 2026

Film by Dust Wave is the text-message program for Film, Dust Wave's private production operations workspace. It sends messages only to invited crew members who explicitly enroll while signed in to their Film workspace.

Dust Wave operates Film. A production title, such as Big Sword, may appear in a message only to identify the crew member's production.

Message categories

  • Call-sheet availability and delivery notices.
  • Production schedule changes.
  • Time-sensitive safety and location updates.

Consent

Crew members enter their own mobile number, choose message categories, and affirmatively accept the disclosure in Film. Workspace membership alone does not enroll a number. Consent is not a condition of employment or participation.

View the Film SMS enrollment form. The published image uses no member or phone information and shows the consent checkbox unchecked by default.

Frequency and charges

Message frequency varies with production activity. Message and data rates may apply. Film does not use this program for advertising, fundraising, investor updates, purchased lists, or unrelated promotions.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out. Reply HELP for help. Crew members can also sign in at film.dustwave.xyz, contact the production coordinator listed in Team, or use the Dust Wave contact page. A later START message does not restore Film consent; the crew member must enroll again inside Film.

Privacy

Film encrypts enrolled mobile numbers and retains bounded consent and delivery evidence. Message content is transmitted to the messaging provider for delivery but is not kept in Film's ordinary audit records. See the Film Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Film by Dust Wave is a private production workspace for invited film teams.
